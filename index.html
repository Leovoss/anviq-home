06 / Engagement

Say who builds, operates and maintains it.

Every engagement starts with a scoped technical assessment before any commitment to build. From there, I quote a fixed scope or an ongoing arrangement, whichever fits the work.

Technical assessment

A paid, fixed-scope review of the workflow and systems involved, ending in a written plan: what to build, what it connects to, what it costs to run.

Build & deploy

Fixed-scope delivery against the plan, deployed into the agreed environment, with documentation handed over at the end.

Ongoing maintenance

A retainer for teams who want the same engineer on call as the system evolves and the systems around it change.

07 / Questions

Common questions.

Who actually does the work? +

I do. Anviq is an independent practice, not an agency with a rotating bench. The engineer you talk to in the assessment is the one who builds and deploys the system.

Where is data hosted? +

Hosting region, access controls and data handling are agreed with you as part of the technical assessment, before implementation starts.

Do I own what gets built? +

Yes. Code, configuration and documentation are handed over as part of delivery, per the agreed scope.

How does a technical assessment start? +

Email a short description of the workflow you want automated or connected, and I will reply with scope and timing for the assessment.

Name the work.

Say what needs building, and I will tell you what it takes to build it.
